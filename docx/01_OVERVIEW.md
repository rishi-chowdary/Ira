# 🪙 IRA — Student P2P Lending Platform
## 01. Platform Overview & Executive Summary

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

**IRA** is India's first campus-to-campus peer-to-peer (P2P) micro-lending network designed specifically for college and university students. The platform bridges the liquidity gap experienced by students through short-term micro-loans funded directly by verified peers, backed by a proprietary trust score (**Shri Score**), automated campus verification, and a double-entry digital wallet (**Batua**).

---

## 💡 The Problem

1. **The Student Credit Gap:**
   - Over 40 million students in Indian higher education have zero formal credit history (no CIBIL score).
   - Commercial banks and NBFCs reject loans below ₹50,000 due to high processing overhead.
   - Predatory loan apps charge exorbitant rates (100–300% APR) and employ aggressive recovery methods.

2. **Frequent Micro-Cash Emergencies:**
   - College life involves continuous, unpredictable small expenses: semester exam fees, mess food charges, books, emergency transit, tech repairs, and event registration.
   - Students typically need between ₹500 and ₹5,000 for periods ranging from 24 hours to 30 days.

3. **Idle Savings with Zero Yield:**
   - Financially comfortable students with monthly allowances keep savings in 0–3% return bank accounts.
   - P2P student lending allows them to safely earn 12–18% annualized returns by helping campus peers.

---

## ⚡ The IRA Solution

- **Campus-Locked Micro-Loans:** Borrow from ₹500 to ₹10,000 for 1 to 90 days.
- **Zero-Interest Emergency Buffer:** 0% interest for repayments made within the first 24 hours (₹0.01 platform fee only).
- **Automated Trust Engine (Shri Score):** A 0–1000 credit score built specifically for students based on repayment discipline, attendance, academic standing, and peer endorsements.
- **85 / 15 Revenue Split:** 85% of regular interest earned goes directly to student lenders; 15% is retained as a platform service fee.
- **College-Verified Ecosystem:** Students are verified against their college database using institutional email addresses, student IDs, and DigiLocker (Aadhaar & PAN).
- **Social Accountability:** Peer guarantors and campus friend circles minimize default rates through organic social trust.

## 👥 Core User Personas

| Persona | Needs & Goals | Platform Experience |
|---|---|---|
| **Student Borrower** | Urgent cash for college emergencies; transparent interest rates; no predatory harassment. | Fast loan request flow, clear interest slab display, flexible repayment methods (UPI / Batua). |
| **Student Lender** | Earn safe returns (12–18%) on idle pocket money; support fellow students. | Marketplace browse, risk tier filters, automated portfolio distribution, escrow returns. |
| **College Authority** | Ensure student welfare; verify bona fide status; prevent illicit campus money-lending. | Dedicated College Portal to view registrations, verify bona fide students, and manage program IDs. |
| **Platform Admin** | Platform stability, regulatory compliance, dispute resolution, financial auditing. | Admin dashboard with live stats, approval queues, loan overrides, and hierarchy exports. |
